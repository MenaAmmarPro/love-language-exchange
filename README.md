# Two Tongues — love-language-exchange

A tiny Urdu ↔ Romanian phrasebook for Mena (Romania) and Ammar (Pakistan).
Eighteen everyday phrases, a flip-card deck, and a little quiz to keep score.

Live: https://menaammarpro.github.io/love-language-exchange/

## How it works

- **The phrasebook** — each card shows the Urdu (with a romanised reading) on the
  front; tap it to flip to the Romanian and English on the back.
- **Little quiz** — read the Urdu and pick the right Romanian; a correct answer
  adds one to your score (saved locally).
- **Send a phrase** — copies a `?word=<slug>` link; whoever opens it sees
  *"Ammar sent you a phrase: ..."* and that card flips over.

## Storage key

- `mena-langx` — `{ score, best }`
